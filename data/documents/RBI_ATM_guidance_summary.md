The RBI publishes guidance on failed ATM transactions, including cases where a customer's account is debited but the ATM does not dispense cash. The applicable response depends on the transaction category and the current official guidance. Investigators should verify the transaction record, available ATM journal/event evidence, reversal or credit status, and relevant transaction dates before preparing a recommendation.
